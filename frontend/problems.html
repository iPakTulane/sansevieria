Common Environmental

Yellow Leaves

Common environmental issue usually caused by overwatering or poor soil drainage.

medical_services

Quick Fix Protocol

1 Isolate

Stop watering immediately. Move to a brighter spot with indirect light.

2 Prune

Cut off yellowing parts with sterilized shears to redirect energy.

3 Aeration

Gently poke holes in the soil using a chopstick to increase oxygen.

Prevention Tip

lightbulb

Sansevieria are succulents; only water when the soil is bone dry. In winter, this might mean once every 4–6 weeks.
