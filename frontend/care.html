Sansevieria trifasciata

Snake Plant

Easy Maintenance
opacity

Watering

Every 2-3 weeks. Allow soil to dry out completely between waterings.

wb_sunny

Light

Thrives in indirect sunlight. Tolerates low light levels very well.

grid_view

Soil

Use a well-draining cactus or succulent potting mix for best growth.

thermostat

Temperature

Ideal range: 65°F–85°F (18°C–29°C). Protect from cold winter drafts.

lightbulb

Expert Pro Tip

Snake plants are excellent air purifiers and can tolerate neglect, making them perfect for bedrooms and office spaces.
